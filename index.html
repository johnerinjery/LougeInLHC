    What this does and does not know

    • Only scheduled classes count. A room shows as free when the timetable has nothing in it. Labs, exams, club bookings and cancelled classes are not in the file, so confirm before you rely on a room.
    • Only rooms in the timetable are listed. A room that never appears in the PDF cannot be reported as free. Mon–Fri only.
    • NLH holds several halls. The timetable lists it as one location, and Year I and Year II sometimes meet there at the same moment. “In use” for NLH means at least one class is on; a hall inside it may still be empty.
    • Short changeovers count as use. Back-to-back classes in one room with 5 minutes or less between them are treated as one block. The 15-minute break after the 08:45 slot and the lunch gap do show as free.
    • Multi-room cells. Where one PDF cell lists several rooms (for example Wednesday 14:00–15:55 for Year I), every listed room is marked in use. LHC 301 on Friday 14:00 is listed for both Year I and Year III & IV.
